Two treatments, two ways of renewing your skin

LaseMD Ultra

A non-ablative thulium fractional laser that treats the skin’s surface in a gentle, controlled way. It is commonly used for uneven tone, sun damage, fine lines and rough texture.

Microneedling

Fine needles create tiny controlled micro-channels that prompt the skin’s natural repair process and support collagen. It is commonly used for texture, acne scarring and loss of firmness.
